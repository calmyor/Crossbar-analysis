Analytical optimum

Maximum SNR appears where two ADC errors balance.

Changing the sensing resistance trades clipping against quantization. Their balance reveals the best attainable compute SNR for the selected device and dot-product dimension.

The optimum is a system coordinate, not the minimum of one isolated non-ideality.

How to read the maximum

Improving one error eventually exposes another.

  • Low sensing resistance. The signal grows, but the ADC clips more often.
  • High sensing resistance. Clipping falls, but quantization becomes more prominent.
  • Balanced coordinate. Neither ADC error dominates, producing the maximum total SNR.
